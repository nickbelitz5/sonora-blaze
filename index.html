About Us

From the grocery store parking lot, to the NARCh Finals and beyond, our small roller hockey community has seen a thing or two.

Our journey began on the pavement behind John's Sierra Market, where a group of guys (and their kids) played street hockey with worn sticks, a ball, and nothing but a love for the game. From those humble pickup games, we grew into a community. Some members of our little family have gone to compete at national tournaments, play college ice hockey, even represent our country on the biggest possible stage — experiences we never could have imagined back then. Being one of those kids from the lot myself, I know now that none of it would have been possible without the support of our community: the families, local businesses, coaches, teachers and volunteers who believed in us, showed up for us, and helped open doors we could not have opened alone. Now, we want to give that support back. By launching a youth travel hockey program, we hope to give the next generation of players the same chance to develop their skills, build lifelong friendships, travel, compete, and discover that big dreams can start anywhere — even behind a grocery store.

The Goal

We’re on a mission to grow the sport of roller hockey across Northern California by creating more opportunities for players to learn, compete, and fall in love with the game. Through youth programs, competitive travel teams, community support, and access to experienced hockey mentors, we aim to build a stronger hockey culture

Our goal is to build a fun, competitive travel hockey program that gives young players in our community the same opportunities, support, and sense of belonging that helped shape our own journey. Getting started would begin with open skates, skills clinics, and local practices designed to welcome players of all experience levels, followed by the formation of travel teams that can compete in regional events and, over time, national tournaments. Along the way, players would have the chance to learn from athletes with professional hockey experience. Players will get firsthand insight into the work ethic, teamwork, and mindset required to compete at a high level. We want to create an environment where anyone can improve their skills, make friends, travel, and experience the excitement of representing something bigger than themselves. Ultimately, our mission is simple: have fun, grow the game, and make a lasting impression in the roller hockey world—
